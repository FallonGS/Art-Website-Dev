Supplies

Here we will get you started with some basic supplies to get you started, regardless of your budget.

Home Page

Basic items to get started with acrylic or oil painting:

  • Canvas: This could be panels, stretched canavas or paint grade paper
  • Brushes: It is good to have a wide variety of brushes
  • Paints: Acrylic, oil or watercolor can work, just make sure you get all primary colors
  • Pallet: This is where the paint will go while your using it
  • Cup/Container:(For water/solvent) To rinse your brush
  • Paper Towls/Rags: To dry your brush between rinses
  • (Optional)Easel: To place your canavs on, for ease of use
  • (Optional)Brush Solvent: If you get oil paint, you will need this!

You can get all of these items from a craft store (like Hobby Lobby), online (Amazon), or most of it you can even find at Walmart or Dollar General. Don't worry yourself too much about getting the "correct" brands, you can always upgrade later if you are on a budget.
