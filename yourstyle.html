How to find your artstyle

Finding your style can be a long road, but identifying your strengths and inspirations is a good start. Bellow you will find some tips on how to discover your unique style!

Home Page

Some things to try while finding your style:

  1. Experiment with styles that you really like. Mix and match elements from these styles to create something unique
  2. Break down different techniques to gain an understanding of them
  3. Look back on your work and find what you like best about it. What elements do you like, and which would you like to change?
  4. Practice! Practice is important to developing your style.
  5. Don't expect perfection; this is a learning process and will take time. Remember to enjoy yourself and don't get too caught up in getting "better"

It may take weeks, months, or years to develop your own unique style, but don't worry. You can do it. Some people continue to evolve their artstyles their whole lives, so remember that you are free to change things up at any time. Most of all, do what makes you happy! Don't worry about what anyone else thing, this is for you. Keep it up!
